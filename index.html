Probability Formulas

  • Probability: \(P(E) = \dfrac{\text{favourable}}{\text{total}}\)
  • Empirical prob.: \(\dfrac{\text{frequency}}{\text{total frequency}}\)
  • Complement: \(P(\text{not }E) = 1 - P(E)\)
  • At least one: \(1 - P(\text{none})\)
  • Combination: \(C(n,r) = \dfrac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}\)
  • Drawing all at once: \(\dfrac{C(a,r)C(b,k-r)}{C(a+b,k)}\)
  • Queue (no adjacent): \(\dfrac{C(B+1,g)\cdot g!\cdot B!}{(B+g)!}\)
  • Independent events: \(P(A \text{ and } B) = P(A)\cdot P(B)\)
  • Turn-taking game: \(P = \dfrac{s}{1-r}\)
